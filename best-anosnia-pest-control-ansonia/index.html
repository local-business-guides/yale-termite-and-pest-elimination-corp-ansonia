Questions worth asking before you hire

Use the first call to get a sense of how the service will fit your situation. You can ask:

  • What information would help you understand the pest issue?
  • What should I expect during the visit and after treatment?
  • Are there items or areas I should prepare before the service?
  • How do you account for children, pets or other household considerations?
  • What should I do if I notice activity again?

You don’t need to identify the pest with certainty before calling. A description of its size, appearance, location and timing can still help. If you have a photo, mention that you can share it, but don’t approach or handle an insect or rodent to get a better look.

A short home-preparation checklist

Before speaking with a provider, write down the rooms or outdoor spots where you’ve noticed activity. Note any changes, such as sightings becoming more frequent or moving to another area. If you’ve already tried a product or trap, tell the provider what it was and where you used it. That context can help prevent confusion about what’s happening.

Make a simple list of questions and keep it nearby during the call. Clear access to the areas of concern may be helpful, but wait for specific preparation instructions before moving belongings or applying another product. Avoid mixing treatments or using a product in a way its label doesn’t allow. Those steps can create unnecessary risk and make it harder to understand the source of the problem.

When professional help is a sensible next step

Consider calling when pests keep appearing, when the source is unclear, or when you’re unsure how to respond safely. It also makes sense to ask for help if activity is affecting more than one room or returning after your own efforts. The right recommendation depends on what’s present and the details of the property, so share what you know and ask what the service can address.
