Questions to ask before you book anosnia pest control in ansonia

A good company won't mind questions. Ask them up front and listen to how they answer.

  • Will a technician look at the property first, or is the treatment decided over the phone?
  • Do you handle both insects and rodents?
  • How do you deal with the reason the pests got in, not just the pests you can see?
  • What products do you use, and what should my family and pets do afterward?
  • Is the technician licensed in Connecticut?
  • Do you do follow-up visits if the problem isn't gone?

If the answers are vague, keep looking. You want someone who explains things in plain words.

A short checklist for choosing a pest company

Before you commit, run through this list:

  • They've been working in this area long enough to know local pests and local housing.
  • They inspect before they treat.
  • They tell you what they found, in terms you can follow.
  • They take your situation into account, whether you own, rent or run a business.
  • They're reachable. You can call and get a person during normal hours.

Older homes around the Naugatuck Valley have different weak spots than newer builds. Gaps around pipes, old foundations and damp basements all invite pests. A company that works locally will check for those without being asked.

Mistakes to avoid

These come up again and again.

Waiting too long. One mouse is rarely just one mouse. Insects build up fast, too. The earlier it's looked at, the simpler the fix.

Spraying everything yourself. Store-bought sprays can scatter ants and roaches into other parts of the house. That makes the problem harder to trace.

Ignoring food and water. If crumbs, open pet food and leaky pipes stay put, pests will keep coming back no matter what gets applied.

Treating only the room you saw them in. Pests travel along walls, pipes and wiring. The nest is often somewhere else.

Hiring on a phone quote alone. Without seeing the space, nobody can really know what's going on.

What to prepare before the visit

A little prep helps the technician work faster and more thoroughly.

  • Write down where and when you've seen pests.
  • Clear access to under-sink areas, basements, attics and closets where you've noticed activity.
  • Put away food, dishes and pet bowls in treated areas.
  • Keep a note of any water leaks or gaps you already know about.
  • Tell the technician about kids, pets, allergies or anyone with health concerns.
  • If you rent, let your landlord know so access isn't an issue.

When it's time to call a pro

Some pest problems are fine for a weekend of cleaning and a few traps. Many aren't. Call a professional if you see pests again after cleaning, if you hear scratching in walls or ceilings, if you find droppings or chewed material, or if you notice signs of wood damage that could point to termites. Business owners should call right away, since pests can affect inspections and customers.
